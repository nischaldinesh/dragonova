How it works

From curious to creating in one evening.

  1. 01Step 01

    Find an event

    Choose a hobby you’ve always wondered about, or one you’ve never heard of.

  2. 02Step 02

    Show up

    Tables, tools, and materials are ready. Grab a drink and settle in.

  3. 03Step 03

    Try it, your way

    Follow simple prompts or get help from a skilled maker. No pressure.

  4. 04Step 04

    Leave with your creation and a real sense of whether it’s your thing.

FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need any experience?

None at all. Events are designed for complete beginners, and experienced hobbyists are welcome too. You don’t have to be good at something to enjoy trying it.

What do I need to bring?

Just yourself. Supplies and tools for the activity are provided. Each event listing notes anything extra worth knowing.

How much do events cost?

It depends on the activity. Some events are free or low-cost introductions; others with more materials or a specialist cost more. The price is always listed clearly before you sign up.

Can I come alone?

Absolutely, plenty of people do. You can focus quietly on your project or chat with others at the table. Both are welcome.

I run a café or teach a craft. How do we work together?

Join the list and choose “Café / venue” or “Creative / teacher.” We’ll reach out to talk about timing, setup, and terms, all agreed clearly in advance.
